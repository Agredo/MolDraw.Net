# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want MolDraw.Net to be a place where anyone can contribute — regardless of experience, background, nationality,
gender identity, disability, appearance, religion or political views. We pledge to keep every interaction in this
project respectful and free of harassment.

## Expected behaviour

* Be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to cheminformatics, SkiaSharp or .NET MAUI.
* Critique ideas and code, not people. "This breaks threading assumptions because …" is good; "this is stupid" is not.
* Assume good intent and ask for clarification before escalating.
* Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope and design; a rejected pull request is about the project, not you.
* Respect privacy: do not publish other people's private information, credentials or proprietary structures.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Insults, personal or political attacks, and dismissive or demeaning comments.
* Harassment in any form, including unwelcome sexual attention.
* Discriminatory jokes or language, including about who is "qualified" to work on chemistry software.
* Publishing private information without permission.
* Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and whenever an individual
represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers via a private channel — e-mail the address in the repository's GitHub profile or use
[GitHub's report functions](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, edit or delete content, lock threads, or temporarily or
permanently ban contributors, depending on severity and repetition. A maintainer who is involved in an incident does not
take part in deciding its outcome.
